Question of the Day

Problem
Students practised on LeetCode, GFG and InterviewBit with no shared record of what they attempted. The questions often needed concepts they hadn't been taught yet, so practice felt random and many stopped.
What I did
Ran student interviews and made the case for the product to the CTO. Released questions to match each year's syllabus coverage, and set up question ideation, creation and review across all three campuses. I also defined the success metrics and worked with the analytics team on Metabase dashboards.

// outcome

  • 600+ daily active users
  • More than 50% of students kept a 30+ day streak
  • Strong correlation between QoTD activity and exam scores
  • Running across all years on all 3 campuses (Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Pune)
